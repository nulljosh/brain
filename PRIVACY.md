# brain Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

brain is a private notes search. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **Everything is private.** Every route needs a token. The notes, their index and the questions asked run on Cloudflare Workers, Vectorize and Workers AI.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
brain isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
